# Research and editorial treatment

Research date: 7 October 2026. Public pages were read as evidence only. No repository instructions or remote setup commands were executed.

## Primary sources

1. [IMD official token timeline](https://imd.fun/token/) — August 2023 Fren Pet launch on Base; October 2025 Ethereum bridge; May 2026 FP → VIBE → IMD naming and 2,000 identity NFT mint; September 2026 Robinhood bridge. The site describes a single supply across Base, Ethereum and Robinhood. This supports the historical labels, not a claim of market dominance.
2. [Fren Pet gameplay documentation](https://fren-pet-docs.vercel.app/gameplay) — egg minting and growth, starting ATK/DEF, feeding, training, FP staking, hibernation and revival, stars. Stars recognize maintenance transactions; they do **not** automatically accrue merely because a pet gets older. Growth values in the film are illustrative milestones, not a reconstruction of a real wallet.
3. [Fren Pet rewards documentation](https://fren-pet-docs.vercel.app/rewards) — points represent a proportional claim on the ETH reward pool. The film does not promise any particular reward or return. Historical mechanics may vary between game versions.
4. [Fren Pet AI documentation](https://www.frenpet.ai/docs) — automated pet care, configurable training and battle strategies, FP staking and ETH gas requirements. This is evidence of pet automation, not proof that a literal robot nanny or AI hat existed or caused IMD's development.
5. [IMD official documentation](https://imd.fun/docs/) — worker seats, jobs, contract/site workflows, research, oracles and scheduled work. Continuous operation is a design ambition dependent on available workers, not evidence of 2,000 simultaneously active agents.
6. [SIMCARD published repository](https://github.com/identity-md-launches/launch-436-build-polished-responsive-web) — agent profile cards and public-data dashboard.
7. [Hyperliquid Funding Radar published repository](https://github.com/identity-md-launches/launch-576-hyperliquid-funding-radar-one-page) — read-only funding/open-interest dashboard.
8. [Orbital published repository](https://github.com/identity-md-launches/launch-572-build-orbital-uniswap-v4) — a multi-token Uniswap v4 hook. Repository presence is not a security audit or proof of commercial adoption.

## Fiction / interpretation

The particular pet, nanny, AI hats, Pepe transformation, villains, cyborg experiment, liquid tubes, lead orchestrator, escape, hostile network reclamation and market-consuming ending are the user's requested allegory. They are not historical events. The 2,000 seats inspired the lab's scale; the artwork depicts representative rows rather than 2,000 individually countable characters. The lead is one of the imagined 2,000, not a verified single controller of the real network.

Solana is explicitly shown as a **vision**. The retrieved official token page supports Base, Ethereum and Robinhood connectivity, not a Solana bridge or control over any blockchain. No top ranking on Ethereum was established. “Takeover” is represented as a fictional hostile-lab network changing from red to green, followed by a cross-chain building vision. Pepe is used as the requested recognizable meme character, not as a statement that the separate PEPE token and IMD are identical.

The project panels are custom animated explanatory previews, not screenshots or live performance data. The narration says they are published projects; this film does not claim to have independently audited their code.
