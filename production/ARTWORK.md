# Artwork and sound provenance

Original storyboard generated using the built-in imagegen tool under the imagegen skill. Originally saved as PNG; the compact editable asset is `production/storyboard.jpg`; its returned resolution is 1448 × 1086. The film crops the twelve panels and scales them into a 1280 × 720 composition. No external photograph or music recording is used.

The installed video prediction tool was tried for a cinematic typing clip using `minimax/video-01`; the request timed out without returning usable video bytes. The delivered film therefore uses locally animated generated art, camera movement, light, particle, glass, HUD, orbit and network effects. It is a motion comic, not fully articulated character animation or filmed live action.

Narration: synthetic English voice `en-US-GuyNeural`, generated once with edge-tts and retained as local MP3 source files. Sentences are individually fitted to their scene with pitch-preserving tempo adjustment. Exact rates and durations are in `narration-timing.json`.

Music and effects: original procedural minor-key synth score, stereo pads, arpeggios, bass pulses, keyboard clicks, UI tones, scene impacts and glass noise. All synthesis is defined in `audio.py`. The original lossless master was used for the delivered MP4; the compact editable mix is `production/soundtrack.m4a` (AAC). The standard-library resynthesis script preserves the composition but uses a different deterministic noise generator. Fonts: bundled DejaVu, with its license.
